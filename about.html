About Me

My name is Sam, and I am a student in ENGL 170: First-Year Writing at Lindenwood University. This blog documents my thinking and writing throughout the semester as I explore questions about AI, authorship, creativity, and intellectual work.

Over the course of the semester, I will use this space to reflect on course readings, respond to classmates’ posts, and experiment with different ways of writing alongside AI tools. These posts are not meant to be polished final arguments, but ongoing engagements with ideas as they develop.

Outside of this class, I am interested in how technology shapes everyday life and how writing can be used to make sense of complex systems. This blog is part of a larger network of ENGL 170 student blogs, and many of my posts will directly link to and respond to work by my classmates.
